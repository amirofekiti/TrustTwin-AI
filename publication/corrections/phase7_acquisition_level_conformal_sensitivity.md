# Phase 7 acquisition-level conformal sensitivity

A fresh five-seed sensitivity analysis used acquisition-grouped splits and evaluated global LAC at both the 12-second segment level and the reconstructed 60-second acquisition level.

At nominal 95%:
- segment-level mean empirical coverage: **0.957**
- segment-level mean review rate: **0.043**
- acquisition-level mean empirical coverage: **0.975**
- acquisition-level mean review rate: **0.025**

Each acquisition-level split used 55 calibration acquisitions and 55 test acquisitions.

No acquisition-level base-classifier errors occurred after sibling-probability averaging in these sensitivity splits. Therefore the sensitivity analysis cannot establish acquisition-level error-interception performance. It supports only the narrower conclusion that near-nominal/conservative coverage persists when the physical acquisition is used as the conformal calibration/evaluation unit.

The corrected Phase 7B segment-level result remains the primary operational analysis and must be interpreted with its clustered physical-acquisition structure stated explicitly.
