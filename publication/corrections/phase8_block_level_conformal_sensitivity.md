# Phase 8 block-level conformal sensitivity

A separate sensitivity analysis used fresh deterministic experimental-block-safe splits and averaged cycle probability vectors within each experimental block before conformal calibration/evaluation.

At nominal 95% block-level LAC:

| Target | Mean empirical coverage | Mean review rate | Mean set size |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Cooler | 0.986 | 0.014 | 0.986 |
| Pump leakage | 0.979 | 0.021 | 0.979 |
| Accumulator pressure | 0.938 | 0.421 | 1.476 |
| Valve condition | 1.000 | 1.000 | 3.862 |

These fresh sensitivity splits do not reproduce the original Phase 8 split selection exactly and therefore do not replace the original headline numbers. They preserve the qualitative interpretation: strong tasks require little review, accumulator performance is less stable, and the weak valve task becomes effectively non-automatable.
