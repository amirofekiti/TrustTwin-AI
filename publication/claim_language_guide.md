# Claim Language Guide

## Strong but supported

- "The order-envelope representation improved robustness across the tested held-speed and held-condition protocols."
- "Severe sensor corruption can produce confidently wrong diagnoses."
- "Predictive entropy was a strong unknown-fault indicator when operating conditions were represented."
- "The same OOD evidence degraded sharply when operating-condition shift was introduced."
- "Global split conformal provided a useful in-domain abstention mechanism in the tested datasets."
- "The independent hydraulic replication showed that review burden can increase as base-task competence decreases."

## Use qualification

- Prefer **approximately nominal empirical coverage** over **guaranteed 95% coverage**.
- Prefer **workflow replication** over **external classifier transfer**.
- Prefer **offline explanation-stability evidence** over **explanation trust score**.
- Prefer **diagnosis may proceed to a human-facing workflow** over **safe diagnosis**.
- Prefer **unknown-fault indicator within represented operating support** over **universal OOD detector**.

## Avoid

- "TrustTwin guarantees safe maintenance."
- "The model is 100% reliable."
- "The conformal layer corrects wrong diagnoses."
- "The explanations reveal causal fault mechanisms."
- "The UCI experiment proves Motor-2 generalises to other machines."
- "GREEN means the diagnosis is correct."
