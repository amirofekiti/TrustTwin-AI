# Datasets

## NLN-EMP

**Motor Current and Vibration Monitoring Dataset for various Faults in an E-motor-driven Centrifugal Pump**

DOI: `10.4121/2b61183e-c14f-4131-829b-cc4822c369d0.v4`

Primary development focused on Motor-2 vibration with held-speed and condition/severity generalisation tests. Electrical and vibration acquisitions are not assumed synchronised.

## UCI hydraulic systems

**Condition Monitoring of Hydraulic Systems**

DOI: `10.24432/C5CW21`

Used for independent workflow replication, not direct transfer of the Motor-2 classifier.

Raw datasets are not redistributed. Download them from the original providers and follow their terms.
