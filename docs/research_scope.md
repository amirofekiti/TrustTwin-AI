# Research scope

## Working title

**TrustTwin AI: An Explainable and Uncertainty-Aware Artificial Intelligence Framework for Fault Detection and Risk-Aware Predictive Maintenance in Industrial Digital Twins**

## Primary research question

How can an explainable and uncertainty-aware AI layer improve trustworthy fault diagnosis and risk-informed maintenance decision-making in an industrial digital twin when sensor quality and operating conditions degrade?

## Core novelty target

A digital-twin trust layer combining explicit operating-domain support, sensor-health evidence, predictive novelty/OOD, conformal abstention and offline explanation stability to decide whether a diagnosis may proceed or should be withheld for human review.

The project intentionally rejects the idea that one confidence number is sufficient evidence of trust.
